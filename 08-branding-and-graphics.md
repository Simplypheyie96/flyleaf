# 08 — Branding, Logo & Graphics

> Feed after 07 (or earlier if you want the identity locked before screens —
> your call). Covers the Flyleaf logo, leaf motif, app icon, share designs, and
> the polished graphics throughout.

## Core idea
Flyleaf's identity: literary, nostalgic, warm, with a signature LEAF motif
(a flyleaf is a book's page; a leaf is also a page). Buttons and logo carry a
subtle leaf styling so the brand feels unique and cohesive.

## Name
Working name: **Flyleaf.** (Confirm trademark/app-store availability before
committing — the word has prior uses. Alternatives in the same spirit if needed:
Marginalia, Dog-ear, Ex Libris, Recto.)

## Logo & leaf motif
- Design a memorable, modern-but-nostalgic wordmark + mark. The mark should read
  as a leaf AND evoke a turning page (leaf = page). Explore a few directions.
- Leaf styling as a system: a recurring leaf detail on the primary button, the
  add "+", bookmarks, and section flourishes — subtle, not stamped everywhere.
- Provide the logo in light + dark, horizontal + icon-only, SVG.

## App icon
- Top-notch PWA install icon (maskable + all required sizes). Warm, atmospheric,
  instantly readable at small sizes; the leaf mark on a candle-glow field.

## Share designs (per content type)
- Different, beautiful share cards depending on what's shared: a quote card, a
  note card, a book card, a "books I loved" collection card. Each on-brand
  (aged paper + one warm accent + leaf flourish), with crisp typeset text
  (never model-rendered). Sharing is opt-in and private-by-default.

## Empty states & flourishes
- Every empty state gets a small, warm illustrated moment (leaf/thread/paper
  motif) that invites the next action.

## Constraints
- All graphics match the app's warm, hand-made, atmospheric aesthetic (light +
  dark variants). Text on any generated graphic is typeset by us, crisp.
- Deliver as reusable assets (SVG where possible) so they're low-maintenance.

## Negative prompt
No glossy/generic AI look. No leaf motif overuse (tasteful, not a pattern spam).
No reference branding. No blurry raster where SVG works. No model-rendered text.

## Success
Flyleaf has a distinctive, cohesive identity; the leaf motif ties buttons, logo,
and icon together; share cards are beautiful and on-brand; no empty state feels
cold; the app icon looks premium on a home screen.
